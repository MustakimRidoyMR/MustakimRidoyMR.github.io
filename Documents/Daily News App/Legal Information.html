6. Coins & Virtual Currency System

6.1 Coins Overview

Coins are our virtual currency system that allows users to unlock premium features within the app.

6.2 Earning Coins

Users earn coins through the following activities:

  • Viewing Advertisements: 1-5 coins per ad view
  • Clicking on Ads: 2-10 coins per click
  • Daily Login: 1-3 coins per day
  • Reading Articles: 1 coin per article (daily limit applies)
  • Sharing Content: 2 coins per share

6.3 Coin Values & Exchange Rates

Current Exchange Rate:

  • 1 Coin = 0.001 USD
  • 1 Coin = 0.10 BDT (approximate, based on current exchange rates)
  • 100 Coins = 0.10 USD = 10 BDT
  • 1000 Coins = 1.00 USD = 100 BDT
Important Notice: Coins are virtual currency for in-app purchases only. Coins cannot be:
  • Converted to real money or cash
  • Withdrawn to bank accounts or payment services
  • Transferred to other users
  • Exchanged outside the app ecosystem

6.4 Spending Coins

Coins can be used to purchase:

  • Ad-Free Experience: 500 coins/month
  • Premium Categories: 200 coins/category/month
  • Advanced Search: 300 coins/month
  • Unlimited Bookmarks: 150 coins/month
  • Priority News Alerts: 250 coins/month

6.5 Coin Balance & Expiration

Coin balances are stored securely on our servers. Coins do not expire, but account inactivity for 12+ months may result in balance reset.
