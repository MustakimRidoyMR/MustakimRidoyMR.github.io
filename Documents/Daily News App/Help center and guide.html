🚀 Getting Started with Daily News App

📥 Download & Installation

  1. Download from Google Play Store: Search for "Daily News App" or use the direct link provided by our team
  2. Install the App: Tap "Install" and wait for the download to complete (approximately 15-25 MB)
  3. Grant Permissions: Allow necessary permissions for notifications, internet access, and storage
  4. Launch the App: Tap the app icon to open Daily News App for the first time
📱 System Requirements: Android 5.0 (API level 21) or higher, 50MB free storage space, stable internet connection

🎯 First Launch Setup

  1. Welcome Screen: Review the app introduction and feature highlights
  2. Language Selection: Choose between English and Bengali (can be changed later)
  3. News Preferences: Select your preferred news categories (World, Tech, Sports, Local, Business, Entertainment)
  4. Notification Settings: Choose if you want to receive breaking news alerts
  5. Theme Selection: Pick Light or Dark mode (auto-detect based on system settings available)

📋 Quick Start Guide

📰 Reading Your First Article

Tap on any news headline to read the summary. Tap "Read Full Article" to visit the publisher's website for complete content.

🔖 Bookmarking Articles

Tap the bookmark icon (📑) next to any article to save it for later reading. Access bookmarks from the bottom navigation.

🔄 Refreshing Content

Pull down on the news feed to refresh and get the latest articles from all 50+ news sources.

📱 Navigation Basics

Use the bottom navigation bar to switch between Home, Categories, Bookmarks, Search, and Profile sections.

⚙️ Initial Settings Configuration

  • Auto-Refresh: Set how often the app should check for new articles (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 3 hours)
  • Data Usage: Enable "Wi-Fi Only" mode to save mobile data
  • Font Size: Adjust text size for comfortable reading (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large)
  • Image Quality: Choose between High, Medium, or Low quality for article images
  • Offline Reading: Enable automatic download of articles for offline access

🔧 Troubleshooting & Common Issues

📱 App Performance Issues

🐌 App Running Slowly

  1. Close Background Apps: Clear other apps from your recent apps menu
  2. Restart the App: Force close Daily News App and reopen it
  3. Clear App Cache: Go to Settings > Apps > Daily News App > Storage > Clear Cache
  4. Free Up Storage: Ensure you have at least 500MB free space on your device
  5. Update the App: Check Google Play Store for available updates
  6. Restart Your Device: A simple restart can resolve many performance issues

💥 App Crashes or Freezes

🔄 Immediate Solutions

  • Force close and restart the app
  • Check available device storage
  • Ensure stable internet connection
  • Restart your Android device

🛠️ Advanced Solutions

  • Clear app cache and data
  • Update to latest app version
  • Check Android system updates
  • Uninstall and reinstall the app

🌐 Connectivity Issues

📶 Internet Connection Problems

ProblemSymptomsSolutions
No Internet Access Articles won't load, "No connection" message Check Wi-Fi/mobile data, restart router, check data limits
Slow Loading Articles load partially, images don't appear Switch to Wi-Fi, reduce image quality, check network speed
Intermittent Connection Content loads sometimes, frequent timeout errors Reset network settings, change DNS, contact ISP
VPN Issues App works without VPN, fails with VPN Try different VPN servers, disable VPN temporarily

🔧 Network Troubleshooting Steps

  1. Test Internet Speed: Use speed test app to verify connection quality
  2. Toggle Airplane Mode: Turn on for 10 seconds, then turn off
  3. Reset Network Settings: Go to Settings > System > Reset > Reset Network Settings
  4. Check Data Usage: Ensure you haven't exceeded your data limit
  5. Try Different Network: Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data

🔐 Account & Login Issues

🚫 Cannot Login to Account

  • Password Issues: Use "Forgot Password" to reset via email
  • Email Not Recognized: Check spelling, try alternative email addresses
  • Account Locked: Wait 30 minutes after multiple failed attempts
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Ensure your phone has correct time/date
  • Email Verification: Check spam folder for verification emails

📧 Email Verification Problems

📬 Email Not Received

  • Check spam/junk folder
  • Add our email to contacts
  • Wait up to 15 minutes
  • Request new verification email

🔗 Verification Link Issues

  • Click link within 24 hours
  • Open link in same device/browser
  • Disable ad blockers temporarily
  • Copy link manually to browser

🪙 Coins & Payment Issues

💸 Coins Not Being Credited

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📱 Is Daily News App completely free to use? ▼

Yes! Daily News App is free to download and use. Basic features like reading news, browsing categories, and basic bookmarking are available without any cost. We offer premium features that can be unlocked using coins (earned through app engagement) or optional in-app purchases. You can earn coins by viewing ads, reading articles, and completing daily activities.

🪙 Can I convert coins to real money? ▼

No, coins cannot be converted to real money or withdrawn as cash. Coins are a virtual currency designed exclusively for unlocking premium features within the Daily News App. They cannot be transferred to other users, exchanged outside the app, or converted to any real-world currency. Think of coins as loyalty points that enhance your app experience.

📰 Do you create or edit the news content? ▼

No, we do not create, edit, or modify any news content. Daily News App is a news aggregation service that collects publicly available RSS feeds from 50+ reputable news sources including BBC, CNN, Reuters, Prothom Alo, and others. We display headlines, brief summaries, and provide direct links to the original publisher's website where you can read the complete article.

🔐 Is my personal data safe and secure? ▼

Yes, we take data security very seriously. We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data transmission, secure password hashing, and follow strict data protection protocols. We comply with international privacy laws including GDPR and CCPA. We never sell personal data to third parties. You can review our complete privacy policy for detailed information about data collection and usage.

📶 Can I use the app without internet connection? ▼

Partially yes! While you need an internet connection to fetch new articles, the app offers offline reading capabilities for bookmarked articles and recently viewed content. Premium users can enable enhanced offline mode which automatically downloads articles for offline reading. The app will sync your reading progress and new bookmarks when you reconnect to the internet.

🔄 How often is the news content updated? ▼

News content is updated continuously throughout the day! Our system automatically checks RSS feeds from all 50+ sources every 15-30 minutes for new articles. Breaking news and urgent updates appear within minutes of publication. You can manually refresh the feed by pulling down on the home screen. Premium users receive priority notifications for breaking news 5 minutes earlier than regular users.

🌍 Which languages does the app support? ▼

Daily News App currently supports English and Bengali languages. You can switch between languages in the app settings. The app interface, menus, and help content are available in both languages. We aggregate news content from sources in both languages, including international English sources (BBC, CNN, Reuters) and Bengali sources (Prothom Alo, Daily Star, BDNews24). We plan to add more languages based on user demand.

📱 What Android versions are supported? ▼

Daily News App requires Android 5.0 (API level 21) or higher. The app is optimized for Android devices and works best on Android 7.0 and above. We regularly test compatibility with the latest Android versions. The app requires approximately 50MB of storage space and works on both phones and tablets with various screen sizes.

🎯 How does the personalized news feed work? ▼

Our AI-powered recommendation system learns from your reading behavior, including articles you read, time spent reading, categories you prefer, and topics you search for. The system gradually improves recommendations while respecting your privacy. You can enhance personalization by setting interests in your profile, bookmarking articles, and rating content. All personalization data stays within your account and is never shared with third parties.

🚫 How can I remove or reduce advertisements? ▼

You can reduce ads by purchasing the ad-free experience using 500 coins per month. Alternatively, you can earn coins by engaging with ads - each ad you watch earns you coins that can be used for premium features. We use respectful advertising with options to skip most ads after 5 seconds. You can also adjust ad personalization settings in your privacy preferences to see more relevant advertisements.

📞 How do I contact customer support? ▼

You can contact our support team through multiple channels: 1) Email us at support@dailynewsapp.com (response within 24-48 hours), 2) Use the in-app feedback feature in Settings > Help & Support, 3) For urgent technical issues, use the "Report Problem" button in the app. We provide support in both English and Bengali. Include your device model, Android version, and app version when reporting technical issues for faster resolution.

🔄 Can I sync my data across multiple devices? ▼

Yes! When you create an account, your bookmarks, reading preferences, coin balance, and settings automatically sync across all devices where you're logged in. This includes your reading history, custom categories, notification preferences, and premium feature purchases. Sync happens automatically when you have an internet connection. You can manually trigger sync by refreshing your profile page.
