Tips for Heroes!

✅ To Be a Great Hero

  • • Aim for strategic bank shots off the walls.
  • • Set up and trigger large combos for extra points.
  • • Pop Fuzzies near the top to drop all the ones below them.
  • • Use the swap button to get the color you need!

❌ Obstacles to Watch Out For

  • • Don't let the stack of Fuzzies reach the bottom line.
  • • Be careful with tricky angles and blocked shots.
  • • Some levels introduce new colors and challenges!
  • • Plan your moves carefully when your shots are limited.
